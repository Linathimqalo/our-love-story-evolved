# Five Years & Still You

Build a beautiful, highly polished, romantic and interactive anniversary website for my partner, Nelo, to celebrate our 5-year anniversary on 12 September 2026.

This website should feel like a deeply personal digital love letter rather than a generic Valentine's Day website.

I previously created a Valentine's Day website for her here:

https://nelo-s-love-story.vercel.app/

Use that previous website as inspiration for the emotional/personal nature of the experience, but do NOT simply recreate it. This should feel like the evolution of that website after five years together: more mature, more emotional, more beautiful, more intentional and more reflective.

THE CORE IDEA

The overall theme is:

"Five Years. A Thousand Memories. And Still You."

The website should take her through an interactive journey celebrating our five years together.

I want her to feel:

loved

appreciated

seen

remembered

surprised

emotional

excited

and hopefully a little embarrassed by how cheesy I am

But most importantly, I want her to understand that I genuinely appreciate the person she is.

She is one of the kindest people I know.

She is incredibly passionate.

She is ambitious and wants to achieve a lot with her life.

She cares deeply about the people and things she loves.

She has a softness and kindness about her that I don't want the website to take for granted.

I also want the website to acknowledge something more difficult:

I have messed up during our relationship.

There have been occasions where I have hurt her, disappointed her, failed to show up properly, or taken things for granted.

I don't want the website to pretend that our five years have been perfect.

Instead, there should be a tasteful and sincere section that communicates something along the lines of:

"I know I haven't always loved you the way you deserved to be loved. I've made mistakes. I've hurt you. I've gotten things wrong. And I can't change those moments.

But I can acknowledge them.

I can learn from them.

And I can keep choosing to become a better man, a better partner, and someone worthy of the love you've given me."

Do NOT make this section depressing or guilt-tripping.

It should feel accountable, sincere and hopeful.

The overall message should be:

"Our story isn't beautiful because it has been perfect. It's beautiful because we're still here, still growing, still choosing each other, and still creating something together."

VISUAL STYLE

The entire website should be heavily inspired by Nelo's personality and things she loves.

Primary aesthetic

Use a sophisticated romantic palette built around:

blush pink

soft pink

pastel pink

dusty rose

deep rose

soft magenta

cream

white

subtle lavender/pink gradients

Avoid making it look childish.

It should feel like:

romantic + dreamy + elegant + playful + magical

rather than simply "pink everywhere."

Use beautiful gradients, soft glows, glassmorphism where appropriate, subtle shadows and elegant typography.

Use a mixture of:

elegant serif typography for emotional headings

clean modern sans-serif typography for supporting text

Animations should be smooth, subtle and intentional.

FLOWERS

Flowers should be a recurring visual motif throughout the website.

Use:

roses

small floating petals

floral borders

subtle botanical illustrations

animated flowers appearing as the user scrolls

I especially want some fractal-inspired flowers.

These can look like mathematically generated / infinitely repeating floral patterns.

They should feel almost magical and organic.

Use them sparingly so they feel special rather than overwhelming.

Some flowers could slowly rotate or gently bloom when the user interacts with them.

Do NOT immediately dump all the content onto the page.

The website should feel like an experience.

Something like:

Year 5 — Still Choosing You

Create placeholders where I can easily add my own:

Make it feel like a collection of memories.

For example:

Support:

MP4

Make the implementation easy for me to replace the placeholder videos.

THE HONEST PART

Create a visually distinct section.

Do not make it overly dramatic.

Heading:

"And because I love you, I need to be honest."

This section should communicate accountability.

The message should be editable by me, but use this as the initial copy:

"I know our story hasn't been perfect.

I've made mistakes.

There have been moments where I didn't show you the love, patience, understanding or care that you deserved.

I know I've hurt you in ways I wish I could take back.

I can't rewrite those moments.

And I don't want to pretend they didn't happen just because today is supposed to be romantic.

What I can do is acknowledge them.

Learn from them.

And keep trying to become better.

Not because I'm trying to become perfect.

But because you deserve a partner who keeps choosing growth.

You have loved me through versions of myself that weren't always easy to love.

And I don't take that for granted.

I love you.

And I want the person standing beside you for the next five years to be a better man than the one who stood beside you for the first five."

End the section with:

"I'm still learning how to love you better. But I'm grateful I get to."

Make this section emotionally powerful but tasteful.

No sad music.

No guilt manipulation.

No excessive animations.

INTERACTION & ANIMATION

The website should feel alive.

Use:

smooth scrolling

fade-ins

gentle parallax

floating petals

subtle particles

blooming flowers

cards that gently move

elegant hover effects

cinematic section transitions

soft pink glows

occasional surprise animations

However:

Do NOT overanimate everything.

The website should remain elegant and performant.

Animations should enhance emotion rather than distract from the memories.

Respect reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

RESPONSIVENESS

This is extremely important.

The website must look beautiful on:

desktop

laptop

tablet

iPhone

Android phones

The mobile experience should be designed intentionally rather than simply shrinking the desktop layout.

Photos and videos must work properly on mobile.

Typography must remain readable.

Buttons must be touch-friendly.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Build this as a polished modern web application.

Use a clean component-based architecture.

Make it easy for me to replace:

photographs

videos

captions

dates

messages

audio

timeline memories

Create a centralized data/configuration structure where possible so I don't have to hunt through components to change content.

Use placeholder assets where my personal photos/videos are not yet available.

Do not fabricate personal photographs or memories.

Make it obvious where I should insert my own media.

Include graceful loading states for images and videos.

Optimize images and lazy-load media where appropriate.

Make sure the website performs well despite animations and media.

MOST IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Do not make this feel like a template.

I want the feeling that:

"Someone spent time making this specifically for me."

It should feel handmade even though it is technically polished.

The combination should be:

Romantic + Personal + Playful + Elegant + Emotional + Slightly Ridiculous

The website should tell one story:

Five years together.

Not a perfect five years.

Not a fairy tale.

A real relationship.

Two people who have grown, made mistakes, laughed, struggled, loved, learned and kept moving forward.

And at the centre of all of it:

her.

Build the experience around that.

Start by creating the full visual structure and interactions with placeholder content/media, then make the architecture easy for me to replace those placeholders with our real photographs, videos and memories.
